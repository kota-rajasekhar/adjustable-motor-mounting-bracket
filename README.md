# Adjustable Motor Mounting Bracket — Fresher Portfolio Project

**Project type:** Independent conceptual mechanical CAD exercise  
**Target roles:** Mechanical Design Intern, GET, Mechanical Engineer Trainee, Junior CAD Engineer  
**Planned tools:** SolidWorks, AutoCAD, MS Excel

## Project objective
Create a conceptual adjustable motor mounting bracket made from a base plate, an upright plate and two gussets. The learning goals are part modelling, assembly definition, engineering drawing preparation and BOM organisation.

> **Honest project status:** This repository includes a prepared design brief, a schematic concept illustration, a report and a planning BOM. These are not proof of completed SolidWorks/AutoCAD work. Add your own native CAD files, drawings and screenshots after creating and checking them.

## Proposed dimensions
| Part | Proposed specification | Qty |
|---|---|---:|
| Base plate | 200 × 150 × 8 mm; 4 × Ø11 holes | 1 |
| Upright plate | 150 × 120 × 8 mm; 4 slots, 11 × 25 mm | 1 |
| Gusset | Nominal 80 × 80 mm triangular profile; 6 mm thick | 2 |

Proposed base-hole centre pattern: 160 × 110 mm. Proposed upright-slot centre pattern: 90 × 70 mm. These are educational assumptions; confirm the actual motor interface before application.
